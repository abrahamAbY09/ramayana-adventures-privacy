Ramayana Adventures: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: Ramayana Adventures collects no personal information, has no accounts, shows no ads, and does not connect to the internet. Everything stays on your phone.

Who we are

Ramayana Adventures is made by abrbuilds. You can contact us at abrbuilds@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, collect, store on any server, or share any personal information: no name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access, device identifiers, or advertising identifiers.

What is saved on your phone

To let a child continue a story, the app saves a small amount of game progress (which chapters were played, the stars earned, and settings such as sound and text size) in the app's private storage on the phone. This information never leaves the phone. Deleting the app, or using Settings → Reset all progress inside the app, removes it.

Internet, permissions and third parties

The app works completely offline and asks for no permissions. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services. The stories, pictures, music and voice-over are all inside the app.

Children

The app is made for children about 5 to 10 years old. Because it collects no personal information, there is nothing to share or sell. It has no chat, no social features, no links to the outside, and no purchases.

Where the text comes from

Lines quoted from the Ramayana come from Ralph T. H. Griffith's public-domain English translation (1870–1874). The voice-over is made with open-source voices. Credits are listed inside the app on the “For grown-ups” page.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds an online feature), the new policy will be published here and the app's store page will say so before the feature is added.

Contact
